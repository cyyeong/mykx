Raise a critter.
Learn its life cycle.

Kids raise a frog, a mealworm beetle, a butterfly or a chicken through its real stages. Every stage needs different care, so the science is the game, and the pet answers in its own words.

Ask your pet Critter Keeper

Can you breathe underwater?FrogletNot now! I am growing lungs so I pop up to breathe air. Please add a rock so I can climb out!
Are you hungry? Want some bran?Mealworm pupaNo thank you! Right now I am a pupa so I do not munch. I just need warm rest while I change into a beetle.
Do you want a cricket?CaterpillarNo thank you, I only munch fresh leaves! I feel a bit hungry, please give me soft leaves to grow big.
What do you eat?HenI love to peck corn and leafy greens in my coop! I am all grown up and can lay eggs to start again.
