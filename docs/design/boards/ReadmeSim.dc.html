Tomorrow's devices, simulated today.

mykx-sim stands in for everything that will feed myKx: the myKx mobile app, home cameras, and peripherals for location, motion and health. It pairs and sends like a real device, through the same API, to prove the protocol end to end and give myKx exactly what a real device would. No real child, home or camera is involved.

mykx-sim.vercel.app, passcode only. Today it plays the phone's places and motion and the home cameras; health and motion peripherals come next, on the same protocol.
