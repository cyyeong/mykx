A world before the first question.

Most learning apps open on a login form. myKx opens on a grand door. A child says their magic name and their own door flies in, while their world spreads over the city: petals for Anime Sky, confetti for Idol Stage, leaves for the Terrarium. It's theirs before they've typed a thing.

Inside, every screen wears that world, and the sounds are synthesised in its instruments.
