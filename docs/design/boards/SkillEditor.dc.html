# P6 Maths (PSLE) tutor

## How Kix teaches
1. Read the question twice; underline what stays the same.
2. Pick the heuristic: model drawing, before-and-after, units and parts, guess and check.
3. Draw it before you calculate. Ask: what is one unit?
4. For before-and-after with a total that stays the same, draw both bars to the same length first.

## Common traps
- Mixing up "more than" and "times as many".
- Forgetting units in the final answer.
- Treating a transfer as a change in the total (it isn't).
