All four Before-After types together: 7 questions, 3.6 marks/paper (appearing in **every** paper, usually as a 2-mark Booklet B item plus one 4-mark Paper 2 item).

## Priority tiers (marks/paper × how teachable in a week)

**Tier 1 — do first, biggest return**
- Units & parts thinking applied to **Before-After ratio** (a very common gap; unlocks FR/PCT word problems worth ~22 marks/paper).
- **Angles & Geometry** (13.4 marks/paper; a finite list of 7 facts — ideal for any learner; reasons earn method marks).
- **Whole-number basics**: rounding (3 of 5 papers), order of operations (4 of 5), common factors/multiples (4 of 5), numerals (5 of 5) — cheap, reliable Paper 1 marks.

**Tier 2 — high marks, rule-based, Paper 2 (calculator helps)**
- **Volume** two-tank pours (6.6 marks/paper, nearly all Paper 2 4–5-markers).
- **Circles** perimeter/area of shaded composites (6.6 marks/paper).
- **Fractions** remainder-concept word problems and fraction operations (13.8 marks/paper — the largest topic; operations are quick wins, word problems ride on Tier 1).

**Tier 3 — steady, mostly reading**
- **Data** (9.8): graph reading is usually fine; drill the True/False/Not-possible logic and 'each division = ?'.
- **Percentage** (7.0): % change (divide by OLD), find original, discount vs $ off.
- Rate/charges tables (round UP), Average (go to TOTAL), Algebra (substitute in brackets), Measurement conversions.

**Tier 4 — light touch only**
- Nets/solids/views, Area on grids, Decimals ordering — 1–2 mark items; a 5-minute recap each is enough.

## Distribution by paper section (what a real 2026 paper looks like)
- Booklet A (18 MCQ): 3–4 whole-number/decimal items, 2–3 fractions, 1–2 measurement/time, 2 angle/geometry (often on a grid), 1 net/solid, 2–3 data (bar/pie/table), 1 percentage, 1 pattern, 1–2 ratio/fraction word problems worth 2 marks.
- Booklet B (12 × 2 marks): pairs of computation items (order of ops, fraction ops, decimals, algebra), 2 angle figures, 1 area/circle figure, 1 charges table, 1–2 ratio/fraction word problems (often before-after or repeated identity), 1 data item (graph, or T/F/NPT).
- Paper 2 Q1–5 (2 marks each): factors/multiples or digits, algebra, average, percentage, one geometry/volume/grid item.
- Paper 2 Q6–15 (40 marks): 2 angle questions (3–4 marks, with an is/is-not part), 1 data question with pie or line graph (4–5), 1 volume tanks (4–5), 1 circles composite (5), 1 pattern (3–5), 2–3 fraction/ratio/percentage word problems (4–5, units & parts, assumption, grouping), 1 algebra/rate (3).
