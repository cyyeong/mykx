# How to answer: a routine for every Paper 2 section

One named routine per section. Teach it on an easy item, then let the learner run it alone on a harder
one. Examples come from this pack's items.

## 1. 综合填空: word class first, then meaning

1. Read the whole passage once. Its mood and its turn (before → after) rule out half the options.
2. Look at what sits right after the blank. A verb after it (___准备了) needs an adverb: 特地, not 特殊／独特／特点.
3. Check the collocation: 关系 is 融洽, a place is 热闹; 人数 is 增加-ed, a level is 提高-ed.
4. **关联词:** find the partner word first (不但 → 而且; …就 → 只要). No partner? Ask whether the two clauses agree (而且) or pull against each other (但是).
5. **成语:** the clause after the blank shows the reaction: 竟然 + ！ → 大吃一惊; 总觉得下次再努力就行了 → 满不在乎.
6. Read the sentence back with your choice in it.

## 2. 词语替换: name the mismatch, then fix it

1. Say what the underlined word really means (冷静 = calm and clear-headed).
2. Say what the sentence needs (a response that shows no interest).
3. Name the kind of mistake: wrong partner (提高 + 人数), near-synonym in the wrong place, the opposite meaning (批评 where the principal is praising), or the wrong sense (发明 a method).
4. Write **one word** that fits both the meaning and the words around it: 冷淡, 增加, 表扬, 采用. Check it reads naturally in the full sentence.
5. Write neatly: a wrong character loses the mark.

## 3. 阅读理解一: scan the practical text

- **Flyers and ads:** read the headings first (日期、地点、可修理的物品、不接受、费用、注意事项). Most questions are answered under one heading. Watch the traps: what is NOT accepted, who pays (物主 = the owner), conditions such as 额满即止.
- **News reports:** the first paragraph (导语) gives who, what and the result; a main-idea question is answered there. Later paragraphs give how, why (often in a quoted speaker's words) and what next.
- **Purpose questions** ("主要目的是什么") are often answered in the same sentence as the rule: 物主须留在现场，与义工一起修理，学习简单的修理技巧.
- Check every option against the text; one that adds a detail the text never gives (an alarm, the police) is wrong.

## 4. 阅读理解二: marks = points

1. Count the marks before reading: 3 marks = three points.
2. Find the paragraph(s) that answer it. Reasons are often spread across two paragraphs (what happened; what the character concluded).
3. Write each point as its own clause, linked by 因为……，所以…… or 一、二、三.
4. Find-a-word: copy the word from the named paragraph exactly.
5. Signposts in informative texts (首先、其次、此外) mark the points a "list three" question wants.

## 5. Meaning, feeling and opinion answers

- **What does this line mean?** Explain each half in your own words, then say which matters more: 音不准（技术）可以练；心不定（意志不坚定、想放弃）才可怕.
- **Figurative phrases** (穿上了绿衣): say what it literally refers to, using the next sentence.
- **Feelings change:** feeling before + evidence, feeling after + evidence, and the change in one line.
- **A character's purpose** (阿伦为什么给“我”看照片): what he wanted the narrator to know, and what he wanted him to do.
- **Opinion:** the author's view from the text first, then your own: specific, doable, with a reason. "爱护植物" alone is too general; "不随便摘花踩草，参加社区花园的义工活动" earns the marks.

## 6. Time plan for 90 minutes

| Part | Marks | Suggested time |
|---|---|---|
| 综合填空 (5) | 5 | 5 min |
| 词语替换 (5) | 10 | 10 min |
| 阅读理解一 (10) | 20 | 20 min |
| 阅读理解二 (10) | 35 | 45 min |
| Check | | 10 min |

Spend time where the marks are: 阅读理解二 is half the paper. In practice inside myKx, one section at a
time is plenty; a full paper is for the printable papers.
