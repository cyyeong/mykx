# G3 Chinese (K320): format and marks

Source: SEAB, *Singapore–Cambridge Secondary Education Certificate General 3 (2027): Chinese Language
华文 (Syllabus K320)*, © MOE 2024, the latest published G3 Chinese format. Section names, instructions
and marks below are SEAB's; the teaching notes are ours. The items in this pack copy the format, never
a paper. From 2027 there is one written sitting for MTL in the SEC, with no mid-year retake.

个人意见和内容的组织 (20) and 语文表达和流利度 (20).

## Lower secondary school papers

Sec 1 and Sec 2 Chinese is assessed by the school: MOE's syllabus names school-based standardised
assessments (mid-year and end-of-year examinations) and weighted assessments during the year, and
encourages alternative tasks (speeches, role-play, online discussion, writing an email to an
organisation). Each school sets its own lower secondary paper, so their sections and marks vary.

This pack prepares for the national components above, at Sec 2 difficulty:

- passages of 250–450 characters rather than full exam length;
- two 综合填空 passages (10 blanks) instead of one, because the national part is only 5 marks and the skill underlies everything else;
- 阅读理解一 on two practical texts (a flyer and a news report), 3 questions each;
- 阅读理解二 on two passages (a narrative and an informative text), 8 questions, 2 or 3 marks each.

If the learner's school paper has a part this pack does not cover (for example a separate 字词 or 成语
section), the tutor should say so to the parent rather than guess at its format.

## Marking open answers

阅读理解二's 10 questions share 35 marks; SEAB does not publish the split. This pack uses 2 or 3 marks a
question and one rubric line per mark.

- **Marks = points.** A 3-mark "why" needs three separate points, often from two paragraphs.
- **Meaning of a line:** explain each half in your own words, then say which matters more and why.
- **Feelings change:** name the feeling before, the feeling after, and the evidence for each.
- **Opinion:** state the author's view from the text first (1 mark), then your own, specific and reasoned.
- **词语替换:** the replacement must fit both the meaning and the words around it (反应冷淡, 人数增加, 张贴海报). The app accepts the expected word and listed alternatives; a correct word that isn't listed goes to a grown-up to confirm.

The app marks multiple choice, 词语替换 and find-a-word answers by code. Open answers are compared with
the model answer and key ideas, and count only after a grown-up confirms the score.
