## Not required by the 2023 syllabus (keep out of questions)

From the syllabus's own notes, by topic:
- **Living things (P3):** recall of the names of specific living things and their characteristics, so questions describe the organism's features.
- **Materials (P3) and Light (P4):** the terms transparent, translucent and opaque (say "allows most / some / no light to pass through"); the law of reflection; the concept of density.
- **Human system (P4–P5):** names of bones and muscles; alveoli; heart chambers and valves; the terms artery, vein and capillary.
- **Plant system (P5):** the terms xylem, phloem and stomata (say water-carrying tubes, food-carrying tubes, tiny openings); the relative positions of the tubes; transpiration pull.
- **Reproduction (P5):** the terms self- and cross-pollination; pollen tube formation; where exactly fertilisation happens in the female system; foetal development and the umbilical cord; vegetative propagation such as stem cuttings.
- **Magnets (P3):** recall of nickel and cobalt as magnetic materials; magnetic shielding and induction.
- **Forces (P6):** the direction of friction on rolling objects such as wheels and balls; the terms air resistance and water resistance.
- **Heat (P4):** recall of how fast specific materials (for example, different metals) conduct heat.
- **Energy conversion (P6):** the terms chemical, gravitational and elastic potential energy ("potential energy" is enough).
- **Cells:** nothing beyond "a cell is a basic unit of life".
