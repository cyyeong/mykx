# PSLE English Paper 2 blueprint (SEAB, from 2025)

Source: SEAB, *PSLE English Language (Syllabus 0001)*, "Implemented from the Year of
Examination 2025", the document SEAB lists on its page *PSLE Formats Examined in 2026*.
The numbers below are SEAB's. Everything else (pacing, archetypes) is this pack's advice.

## Paper 2 at a glance (SEAB, from 2025)

**Paper 2: Language Use and Comprehension.** 90 marks, 45% of the subject, 1 h 50 min.
25 multiple-choice questions (Booklet A) and 50 open-ended questions (Booklet B).

| Booklet | Component | Item type | Items | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Grammar | MCQ | 10 | 10 |
| A | Vocabulary | MCQ | 5 | 5 |
| A | Vocabulary Cloze | MCQ | 5 | 5 |
| A | Visual Text Comprehension | MCQ | 5 | 5 |
| B | Grammar Cloze | OE | 10 | 10 |
| B | Editing for Spelling and Grammar | OE | 10 | 10 |
| B | Comprehension Cloze | OE | 15 | 15 |
| B | Synthesis / Transformation | OE | 5 | 10 |
| B | Comprehension OE | OE | 10 | 20 |
| | **Total** | | **75** | **90** |

Paper 2 assesses two objectives (SEAB): **AO1** show understanding of a variety of
written and multimodal texts at the literal, inferential and evaluative levels; **AO2**
use vocabulary, grammar, punctuation and spelling appropriately in context.

The whole subject: Paper 1 Writing (Situational Writing 14, Continuous Writing 36;
50 marks, 25%, 1 h 10 min), Paper 2 (90 marks, 45%), Paper 3 Listening Comprehension
(20 MCQ, 20 marks, 10%, about 35 min), Paper 4 Oral Communication (Reading Aloud 15,
Stimulus-based Conversation 25; 40 marks, 20%). Total 200 marks.

## What changed in 2025

Compared with the format examined up to 2024 (Paper 2 then had 95 marks):

* **Visual Text Comprehension** fell from 8 MCQs to **5** (Booklet A is now 25 marks).
* **Editing for Spelling and Grammar** fell from 12 items to **10**.
* Paper 2 is now **90 marks (45%)**; Oral Communication rose to 40 marks (20%) and
  Paper 1 is 50 marks (Situational Writing 14, Continuous Writing 36).
* Every other Paper 2 component kept its items and marks: Grammar 10, Vocabulary 5,
  Vocabulary Cloze 5, Grammar Cloze 10, Comprehension Cloze 15, Synthesis /
  Transformation 5 items for 10 marks, Comprehension OE 10 items for 20 marks.

Older practice books may still show 8 visual-text questions or 12 editing items. Practise
the current numbers.

## Pacing for 1 hour 50 minutes

| Minutes | Do |
|---|---|
| 0–20 | Booklet A: 25 MCQs, under a minute each. Star anything slow and move on. |
| 20–40 | Grammar cloze and editing (20 items) |
| 40–60 | Comprehension cloze (15 blanks: read the whole passage first) |
| 60–72 | Synthesis / Transformation (5 sentences) |
| 72–100 | Comprehension OE (read once fully, then hunt for each answer) |
| 100–110 | Starred questions, then the ten-minute check in `answer-strategies.md` §9 |

## Archetypes that repeat

**Grammar (MCQ):** past perfect with *by the time / before / after*; present perfect with
*since / for / already*; agreement with *neither … nor, either … or, each of, the number of,
a pair of*, uncountable nouns; question tags after *nobody, hardly, never, let's, I am*;
conditionals (*if + had + past participle → would have*); relative pronouns (*whose, whom*);
gerund or infinitive after verbs (*avoid doing, agree to do*); passive voice.

**Vocabulary (MCQ):** clue-led word choice (contrast and cause signals), phrasal verbs,
idioms and fixed expressions, nearest meaning.

**Vocabulary Cloze (MCQ):** four options per blank, decided by the paragraph's meaning and
by collocation (*draw up a roster, a parched field*).

**Visual Text Comprehension (MCQ):** main purpose and audience; conditions (age, number of
items, deadline, what is free and what costs extra); which statement is NOT true;
inference from layout and wording.

**Grammar Cloze (OE, word box):** relative pronouns, connectors, prepositions in fixed
phrases, reflexive and possessive pronouns, determiners. Each word in the box is used once.

**Editing for Spelling and Grammar (OE):** each marked word is wrong. Spelling: double
letters, *ie/ei*, silent letters, suffix rules. Grammar: tense consistency, agreement,
word form, prepositions.

**Comprehension Cloze (OE, no word box):** one word per blank, often a function word
(preposition, connector, pronoun, helping verb, article) and sometimes a content word the
passage's meaning makes clear.

**Synthesis / Transformation (OE, 2 marks):** *despite / in spite of, although, unless,
no sooner … than, hardly … when, not only … but also, neither … nor, too … to, so … that*;
direct and reported speech; active and passive; relative clauses; comparatives.

**Comprehension OE (OE, 1–2 marks):** literal retrieval, inference (why, how do we know,
what it shows about a character), word or phrase meaning, referents, feelings with
evidence, true or false with a reason, sequencing.

Archetype weights in `skill.json` share each component's SEAB marks among these patterns;
the split inside a component is this pack's estimate.
