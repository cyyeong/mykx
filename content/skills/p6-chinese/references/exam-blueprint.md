# PSLE Chinese (0005): format and marks

Source: SEAB, *PSLE Chinese Language (0005)*, "For Examination from 2017", the document SEAB lists under
*PSLE Formats Examined in 2026*. Section names, instructions and marks below are SEAB's; the teaching
notes are ours. The items in this pack copy the format, never a paper.

## The 200 marks

| Paper | What | Time | Marks | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 试卷一 写作 | 命题作文 or 看图作文, choose one of two; ≥ 100 字; the SEAB-approved dictionary is allowed | 50 min | 40 | 20% |
| 试卷二 语文应用与阅读理解 | four parts, 40 questions (below) | 1 h 40 min | 90 | 45% |
| 试卷三 口试 | 朗读篇章 20 · 会话 30 (the examiner talks with the pupil about a short video); 10 min beforehand to read the passage and watch the video, as often as they like | about 10 min | 50 | 25% |
| 试卷三 听力理解 | 10 multiple-choice questions on 对话、通告、广告、说明、介绍、故事 | about 30 min | 20 | 10% |

The oral is an e-examination. Oral plus listening is 35% of the subject: as much as all of Paper 2's
multiple choice. Practise both at home, out loud.

## Marking open answers

阅读理解二's 11 questions share 32 marks, about 3 marks a question on average; SEAB does not publish the
split. This pack uses 2 to 4 marks a question and one rubric line per mark. Teach "marks = ideas":

- **Two marks, two ideas.** A 2-mark "why" needs the cause and the reason behind it; a "which two" question needs two items.
- **Copy, then fix.** Lifting the exact phrase from the passage is allowed; change 我／你 to the right name (a note from 林老师 says 告诉我 → 告诉林老师).
- **Full sentences.** Begin with the question's own words: 外公不愿意，因为…….
- **Character questions:** one trait word (勤劳、重情义) plus an example that really shows it. An example that proves a different trait earns nothing beyond the trait mark.
- **Find-a-word questions:** copy the word exactly from the named paragraph; a wrong character loses the mark.
- **书面互动:** the reply must cover every point the 实用文 asks for, in the right form (称呼、问候、内容、祝语、署名、日期) and register (polite to a teacher). In this pack's rubric a missing required point loses its mark, however well the rest is written.

The app marks multiple choice, find-a-word and other exact short answers by code. Open answers are
compared with the model answer and the key ideas, and count only after a grown-up confirms the score.
