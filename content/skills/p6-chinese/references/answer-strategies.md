# How to answer: a routine for every Paper 2 section

One named routine per section, short enough to say aloud before starting. Teach the routine with an
easy item, then let the learner run it alone on a harder one. Examples come from this pack's items.

## 1. 语文应用: say it, then test each option

1. Read the whole sentence first, saying 空格 at the blank. The meaning of the gap comes from the words around it.
2. **Pinyin:** say the word aloud in a phrase you know (整理 → 整 is zhěng). For a 多音字, the meaning picks the sound: 着急 zháo, 看着 zhe, 着陆 zhuó.
3. **字:** settle the sound, then the radical: 扣分 needs the hand 扌 (taking away), not 叩 (口, knocking).
4. **词 and 短语:** put each option into the sentence and ask what it usually goes with. 设施 is 齐全, people are 齐心, rows are 整齐. 提高效率, 实现愿望, 克服困难, 达到目标.
5. **关联词:** find the partner word first: …也 → 即使; …就 → 只要／一; …但是 → 虽然; …而且 → 不但.
6. **成语:** find the clue in the sentence (一会儿……一会儿…… → 三心二意). Cross out the two options that mean the opposite.
7. **病句:** check three things in each sentence: is there a subject (通过……使…… hides it)? does the verb fit the noun (改进水平 ✗)? is anything said twice (大约……多 ✗)?
8. **句意:** a question that expects "no" (哪里是……, 难道……吗) means the opposite of its surface.

## 2. 短文填空: the words around the blank

- Read the whole passage once before choosing anything: the story's mood (happy, worried) rules out half the options.
- The options for one blank are usually one word class (all adverbs, all ……心 words). Decide by the words right before and after: 又累又___ + 一点儿也不好玩 → 无聊; 几个月过去了……___长大了 → 渐渐.
- Read the sentence again with your choice in it. If it sounds like something a teacher would say, keep it.

## 3. 阅读理解一: question first, then find

- Read the question and underline its key words (为什么, 心情, 帮助, 主要).
- Find the sentence in the passage that has those words; the answer is usually there or in the next sentence.
- **Feeling questions** give the feeling in other words: 不服气 in the passage, 不情愿 in the options.
- **Main-message questions:** the last paragraph, often the last sentence, says it; the right option says it in other words. Options that are only a detail ("attack from the right") are traps.

## 4. 完成对话: read the line after the blank

- Cover the options. Read the line before and, above all, the line **after** the blank: it is the reply.
- Ask "what was said to get this reply?" A reply with a time (早上八点开始) means the blank asked about time; 没问题 means the blank was a request; 好，星期六见！ means the blank suggested a meeting.
- Then pick the option that fits; reject one that contradicts a later line (我星期六要去外婆家 vs 星期六见).

## 5. 阅读理解二: find, copy, fix the pronoun, count the marks

1. **Count the marks:** 2 marks = two ideas. Say how many ideas you need before you start writing.
2. **Find** the sentence that answers it. A question that names a paragraph (从第一段中找出……) is only answered in that paragraph.
3. **Copy** the key phrase, then **fix** the pronoun (你的 → 小文的; 我 → 林老师).
4. Answer in a **full sentence** that starts with the question's words.
5. **Why questions** often need the hidden reason as well as the stated one: 外公 says only 老顾客怎么办？; the last paragraph says why they matter (情谊).
6. **Character questions:** trait word + example from the text that proves it.

## 6. 书面互动: every point, in the right form

- List what the 实用文 asks for before writing (林老师 asks for 书名 and 推荐的原因). Each point is a mark.
- Use the email form: 称呼 (林老师：), 问候 (您好！), the content, a closing wish (祝您工作顺利！), 署名 and 日期.
- Match the register: 您 to a teacher, polite and short. Meet the length (不少于40个字) with content, not repetition.
- Read it back once: every required point there? names and pronouns right?

## 7. Time plan for 100 minutes

| Part | Marks | Suggested time |
|---|---|---|
| 语文应用 (15) | 30 | 15 min |
| 短文填空 (5) | 10 | 8 min |
| 阅读理解一 (5) | 10 | 10 min |
| 完成对话 (4) | 8 | 7 min |
| 阅读理解二 (11) | 32 | 50 min |
| Check | | 10 min |

Do the multiple choice in order and briskly, mark the unsure ones, and come back. The open answers are
where time runs out: start them by the 40-minute mark. In practice inside myKx, one section at a
time is plenty; a full paper is for the printable papers.
