# Exam blueprint: P3 Chinese end-of-year

Sources: recent school end-of-year papers, tables of specifications and assessment plans
(kept as private reference), and the MOE *小学华文课程标准 2024*. The 2026 P3 cohort (P1 in
2024) is the first P3 on the 2024 syllabus; the paper format did not change, but 书面互动 items (便条、短信、贺卡) now appear in comprehension.

## The 100 marks

| Component | Marks | Time | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 口试 Oral | 30 | ~10 min | 朗读短文 10 · 看图说话 10 · 会话 10 |
| 听力 Listening | 10 | ~20 min | 4 MCQ pinyin/picture matching + 6 MCQ on short texts |
| 作文 Paper 1 | 15 | 40 min | 看图写段: 4 pictures, 2 helping words each, ≥ 50 字 |
| 语文应用与阅读理解 Paper 2 | 45 | 60 min | 8 sections below |

Paper 2, in exam order (schools vary the section numbering, not the content):

| # | Section | Items × marks | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 辨字测验 character discrimination | 5 × 1 | MCQ, 3–4 look-alike/same-sound characters |
| 2 | 词语选择 vocabulary | 4 × 1 | MCQ, 3–4 words |
| 3 | 看图选词 picture → word | 4 × 1 | picture + sentence + 4 options, OR a 6-word bank with 4 pictures, OR line-matching |
| 4 | 词语搭配 collocation | 5 × 1 | 6-word bank, 5 blanks |
| 5 | 组句成段 sequencing | 1 × 5 | 5–7 sentences; first/last may be given |
| 6 | 扩写／仿写／组合句子 | 5 × 2 | write the whole sentence out |
| 7 | 阅读理解一 | 3 × 2 | passage (~150–200 字) or 便条, 3 MCQ |
| 8 | 阅读理解二 (理解问答) | 3 × 2 | passage, 3 open questions |

**Where the marks really are.** Sections 1–4 (18 marks) are vocabulary recall from the
15-lesson catalogue — pure retrieval practice territory. Section 6 (10 marks) is pattern
knowledge: six connector patterns and the 把字句 cover almost every question ever set.
Sections 7–8 (12 marks) reward one technique: find the sentence, copy it, adjust the pronoun.
Oral (30!) is the most under-practised component in most homes and the easiest to lift.

## Technique, section by section

### 1 辨字测验 — "look at the radical, then the sound"
1. Read the whole sentence first; the blank's meaning is decided by the words around it.
2. Say the target word aloud: 沙__ → 沙滩 shā tān. Now you know the *sound*.
3. Among the options with that sound, pick by *radical*: beach = water 氵 → 滩. Straw = bamboo 竹 → 管. Rubbish = earth 土 → 垃圾.
4. Trap: an option that is the *right sound, wrong radical* (难／滩, 官／管／馆, 元／圆／原). Teach the radical story for each catalogue character the moment the learner confuses it.

### 2 词语选择 — "which word fits the frame"
- Connectors come in pairs: 虽然…但是／还是／仍然 · 如果…就 · 只要…就 · 因为…所以 · 不但…而且 · 一…就. Spot the first half, the second half is forced.
- 果然 = as expected (something was predicted); 仍然 = still; 应该 = should; 结果 = as a result.
- Request family: 请求 (child → adult, polite) · 要求 (demand) · 请问 (opening a question) · 需要 (need).
- 准 family: 准时 on time · 准备 prepare · 对准 aim at · 水准 standard.
- 护 family: 保护环境／动物 · 爱护公物／课室 · 照顾人／宠物 · 保卫国家.

### 3 看图选词 — "name the picture before reading the options"
Cover the options, say what the picture shows in Chinese (垃圾桶！), then find it. Distractors are
same-category (瓶子／纸盒／袋子) so the picture decides, not the sentence.

### 4 词语搭配 — fixed phrases, learn as chunks
The exam's favourite pairs (from the bank in `question-bank.md` §6): 全身发抖 · 冒出冷汗 ·
竖起拇指 · 引来蚊虫 · 发出响声 · 保卫国家 · 参观画展 · 改掉习惯 · 搬动椅子 · 废物利用 ·
急忙离开 · 游戏时间 · 照顾宠物 · 升国旗 · 看地图 · 打扫厨房 · 保护环境 · 使用环保袋.
Do the ones you are sure of first, then the leftovers pair up by elimination.

### 5 组句成段 — "find the signposts"
1. The given first/last sentences set the frame (if none are given, the one with a time phrase 一天／上个星期六 starts; the one with 最后／这一天／从那天起 ends).
2. Signpost words fix the middle: 首先 → 接着／然后 → 最后; 有一次 opens an incident; 突然 is a turn.
3. Pronouns point backwards: a sentence starting with 他／它／听了他的话／小狗听到了 must follow the sentence that introduces that person or speech.
4. Read the finished paragraph aloud once — if it sounds wrong, it is wrong.
Marking: usually 1 mark per correctly placed sentence; an entire reversal still earns the ones that land right.

### 6 扩写／仿写／组合句子 — ten marks for six patterns
| Pattern | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 扩写 insert word | adjective (…的) goes before the noun; adverb (…地／不知不觉／突然) goes before the verb | 懂事的小乐…；小狗摇着尾巴跑… |
| 虽然…但是… | obstacle first, then what happened anyway; 还是 may stay | 虽然天气很冷，但是我们还是去游泳。 |
| 如果…就… / 只要…就… | condition first; drop the repeated subject after 就 | 如果你好好学习，就能取得好成绩。 |
| 因为…所以… / X，因为Y | cause and effect either order | 妈妈在房间休息，因为她病了。 |
| 或者 / 却 | 或者 joins two choices after the same verb; 却 sits before the second verb and needs the same subject | 可以看书或者画画；喜欢小猫，却不会照顾小猫 |
| 三句合一 | say the owner once, list the parts with commas, keep each 的 | 兔子的耳朵尖尖的，眼睛大大的，尾巴短短的。 |
| 把字句 仿写 | 谁 + 把 + 东西 + 怎么样了 | 弟弟把玩具收拾好了。 |
Marking: 2 marks = correct structure (1) + every other character copied correctly and the sentence complete with 。(1). A missed 。or a changed character costs the second mark.

### 7 阅读理解一 (MCQ) — "the answer is in the passage, not in your head"
- Underline the question's key words (who / when / why / 对 / 不对), find the same words in the passage, read that sentence and the one after it.
- "以下哪一个句子是不对的" — three options are copied from the passage; the false one changes one detail (塑料袋 → 环保袋, 小兔的家 → 自己的家). Check each option against the text.
- "X 指的是什么" — look just before X for 原来是／好像／像.
- 便条 format: receiver at the top, writer + date at the bottom; questions ask who-to-whom, what/when/where, and how to get something. 短信 completion wants the exact word from the note.

### 8 理解问答 (open) — the copy-and-adjust method
1. Find the sentence that answers the question. Copy it. Change 我／你／它 to the person's name and drop quotation marks — the marker wants a sentence that stands alone.
2. **Marks = ideas.** 2 marks = two details (what + where; who + when). Write both.
3. Templates:
   - 为什么 → 因为……。
   - 从哪里可以看出 → 从“……”可以看出。(copy the evidence sentence inside the quotation marks)
   - 有没有／吗 → first word is the verdict (有／没有／喜欢／爱), then the evidence.
   - 应该怎么做／以后会怎么做 → 他应该／会……，因为……。
4. Never leave a blank. A copied sentence that is half-right still scores 1.

### 作文 Paper 1 — 看图写段 (15)
Structure: 4 pictures = 4 short paragraphs, or one paragraph with a sentence per picture.
Opening formula: 时间 + 人物 + 地点 + 做什么 (今天早上，小明在家里……).
Use the helping words — they are free vocabulary and the marker looks for them. Add one connector
per picture (突然、这时、于是、最后) and one feeling sentence at the end (他觉得很开心／他决定以后……).
Count: ≥ 50 characters; punctuation marks take their own square; indent 2 squares per paragraph.

### 口试 Oral (30)
- 朗读 10: fluency, accuracy, expression. Practise with a finger under each 。— stop fully. Circle the 5 hardest characters before reading.
- 看图说话 10: 5W frame — 什么时候／谁／在哪里／做什么／怎么样；then 你觉得……对不对？为什么？ Aim for 6–8 sentences with 先……接着……最后.
- 会话 10: answer + example + feeling. Two or three sentences per question. Eye contact, no English fillers (say 嗯……让我想一想 instead).

### 听力 (10)
4 pinyin/picture-matching items (hear a word, pick the picture or the pinyin) + 6 MCQ on 2–3 short texts.
Read the options before the audio plays; the second playback is for checking, not for a first listen.
