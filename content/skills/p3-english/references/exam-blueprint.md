# P3 English Paper 2 blueprint

P3 papers are set by each school, not by SEAB, so formats differ a little.
This blueprint is built from tables of specifications that schools published
plus recent school papers (kept as private reference). All follow the MOE English
Language Syllabus 2020 taught through STELLAR 2.0.

**First thing to check:** does the learner's profile record the school's own
section list (from its table of specifications or a recent WA paper)? If yes,
match it exactly.

## Timing in 2026

Most schools ran Oral (Aug–Sep), Composition and Listening (5–8 Oct), and
**Paper 2 in the last two weeks of October**. So in late October, Paper 2 is
usually the only English paper left. The learner's exact date is in their profile.

## Paper 2 at a glance: 50 marks, 1 h 15 min

| Section | Marks | Seen in | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammar MCQ | 8 (some 10) | all | tenses, SVA, pronouns, demonstratives, quantifiers, prepositions, connectors, tags |
| Vocabulary MCQ | 6 | all | context clues, similes, phrasal verbs, collective nouns |
| Visual text MCQ | 5–6 | about half | poster/flyer; Booklet A at many schools |
| Grammar cloze 1 (word box) | 4 | all | very often **personal pronouns** |
| Grammar cloze 2 | 4 | all | very often **subject-verb agreement** (underline the word) or verb forms/prepositions with a box |
| Vocabulary cloze or comprehension cloze | 4–5 | most | with a word box, or open (no helping words) |
| Editing | 3–6 | all | spelling (bold), grammar (underlined), punctuation (circle) |
| Synthesis / sentence combining / word order | 2–4 | most | 1–2 marks each |
| Comprehension 1 | 8 | all | ~180–220 words |
| Comprehension 2 | 8–10 | all | ~220–260 words |

Booklet A (MCQ, shaded on an OAS with 2B pencil) usually holds grammar,
vocabulary and visual text. Booklet B holds everything written.

## Suggested pacing for 75 minutes

| Minutes | Do |
|---|---|
| 0–15 | Booklet A MCQs (about 1 minute each; skip and star anything slow) |
| 15–35 | Cloze passages, editing, synthesis |
| 35–65 | Two comprehensions (15 minutes each: read once fully, then hunt) |
| 65–75 | Starred questions, then the check routine in `answer-strategies.md` |

A 9-year-old will not track this by the clock. Teach it as three checkpoints
instead: "MCQs done by the time a quarter has gone, cloze done by half-time,
then reading."

## Archetypes that repeat (use these to generate new items)

**Grammar MCQ**
* Interrupted past action: *___ when + short past event* → was/were + -ing.
* Happening now: *"Keep quiet. The baby ___."* → is sleeping.
* Habit: *every day / usually / always* → simple present, with -s for one.
* Past marker elsewhere in the sentence: *last year, earlier today, yesterday*.
* Bossy helpers: *has to / must / can / will / did / does* + base verb.
* SVA traps: *each / every / one of the*, *many pails of sand were*, *this helps*.
* Demonstratives with a near/far clue: *over there, on the far table, pointing at the next shelf*.
* Quantifiers with countable vs uncountable: *many hours / little money / some water*.
* Reflexive matching the doer: *students → themselves, us → ourselves*.
* Possessive standing alone: *I did not bring mine*.
* Question tags: flip positive/negative, mirror the helper and tense.
* Prepositions of movement: *into, off, across, through, around*.
* Connectors: *and, but, so, because, although, while, before, after, then*.

**Vocabulary MCQ**
* Clue-twin: the sentence contains a phrase that means the blank (*like wet jelly → slimy*).
* Movement verbs: scamper, stagger, stumble, trudge, skip, dash, tiptoe, stroll.
* Feelings: grateful, astonished, frustrated, invincible, anxious, relieved.
* Similes: as wise as an owl, as fast as lightning, as quiet as a mouse, as busy as a bee, as cold as ice, as brave as a lion, as light as a feather, as proud as a peacock.
* Phrasal verbs: put out, turn down, break down, speak up, look after, give up, run out of, set off.
* Collective nouns: herd, flock, school, pack, swarm, pride, bunch, team.

**Visual text**: organiser ("presents"), date/time/venue, conditions (age, price,
"by" date, early bird), which contact for which purpose, "which is NOT true",
main purpose.

**Comprehension question types**:
literal, true/false (sometimes with reason), sequencing, word meaning (A)/(B),
find a word with the same meaning, referent, phrase lift (exact word count),
feelings + reason (2m), two things (2m), inference/lesson/character.

## Depth limits: what P3 Paper 2 does NOT test

Keep sessions inside these. Teaching beyond them costs focus the learner cannot spare.
* No relative pronouns in synthesis (who/which joining) at most schools; P4.
* No reported speech transformation, passive voice, or conditionals beyond simple *if*.
* No "neither/nor", "unless", "not only... but also" synthesis.
* No present perfect continuous or past perfect production (recognise only).
* No figurative language beyond similes and common idioms.
* Comprehension answers are lifted or lightly rephrased; no extended evaluation.
