# Exam-eve keyword sheet
Read it out loud once. Cover a line and say it from memory. That's it — then sleep!

## 1 · Living things go "GRR + AFW"
- **G**row · **R**espond to changes · **R**eproduce · need **A**ir, **F**ood, **W**ater to survive.
- Taller over weeks → **grow**. More of them → **reproduce**. Moves away when touched / folds leaves → **respond to changes**. Died, lid had no holes → **needs air**.
- Non-living traps: **fire, cloud, robot, car**. A **seed is living**. Moving is NOT a characteristic.

## 2 · Plants, fungi, bacteria
- **Flowering** plants: flowers → fruits → **seeds** (balsam, rice, water lily).
- **Non-flowering**: ferns & moss → **spores**.
- **Fungi** (mould, mushroom, yeast): **NOT plants**, **cannot make their own food**, spores. Mould needs **water, warmth, food, air**.
- **Bacteria**: only seen with a **microscope**; some useful (yoghurt), some harmful (disease). Yeast (not bacteria) makes bread rise.

## 3 · Animal groups — "Fat Alligators Rarely Bite Mean Insects"
- **Fish**: gills, fins, scales, water. **Amphibians**: **moist skin**, land + water (frog, salamander).
- **Reptiles**: **dry scaly skin** (snake, crocodile, turtle). **Birds**: **feathers**, beak (penguin can't fly!).
- **Mammals**: **hair/fur**, **give birth to young alive**, milk (bat flies, whale swims!).
- **Insects: 3-6-2** → 3 body parts, 6 legs, 1 pair of feelers. Spider = 2 parts, 8 legs → NOT an insect.
> Group by the feature in the question — not by where it lives.

## 4 · Materials
- 7 materials: **ceramic, fabric, glass, metal, plastic, rubber, wood**.
- **Strong** = holds heavy things without breaking. **Flexible** = **bends without breaking** (not "soft").
- **Waterproof** = does **not let water pass through**. **Absorbent** = soaks up water → **least water left = most absorbent**.
- **More light units / see clearly = lets more light through** (windows, face shield). Curtains for a dark room → **least** light.
- Top of the water = **floats**; bottom = **sinks**. Never write "because it is light".
> Most suitable? Job → property → material that has ALL the properties needed.

## 5 · Life cycles
- Plant: **seed → germinating seed → young plant → adult (flowers, fruits)**. Germination needs **Water, Air, Warmth** — NOT light.
- **4 stages** (egg → **larva → pupa** → adult): **butterfly, beetle, mosquito**. Young never looks like adult.
- **3 stages**: cockroach & grasshopper (egg → **nymph** → adult, nymph looks like adult); chicken (egg → chick → adult); **frog** (egg → tadpole → frog, tadpole does NOT look like frog).
- **Larva eats**, **pupa does not eat**. Larva/nymph **moults** (sheds skin). Mosquito egg, larva, pupa live in **water**.
- Life cycle = **repeated pattern** — no first or last stage. Draw it as a loop with arrows.
- **"After hatching"** = larva + pupa (not egg, not adult). **"On Day N"** = add bars from Day 0.
- Stop mosquitoes: remove **stagnant water**; oil blocks the larva's **breathing tube**.

## 6 · Magnets
- Two poles, **strongest at the poles** (ends; ring/button magnet = flat faces).
- **Like poles repel, unlike poles attract.** Floating gap = **both are magnets**.
- **Only a magnet can be repelled.** Attracted could just be a **magnetic material**.
- Magnetic: **iron, steel, nickel, cobalt** ("I Never Steal Cookies"). NOT: aluminium, copper, wood, plastic, glass.
- Freely hanging magnet / compass → points **north–south**.
- Pole puzzles: at every touching join the letters **flip**.
- Stronger magnet = more clips / attracts from **further away** / holds more sheets.
- **Stroke method**: one pole, **one direction**, lift away; **more strokes = stronger**. Back-and-forth = weak.
- **Electromagnet**: coil of wire on iron/steel + battery. **More turns or more batteries = stronger.** Switch off → drops things.

## 7 · Answering — CER: Say it → Show it → Science it
- **Claim**: the answer (start with Yes/No if asked "Do you agree?").
- **Evidence**: point at the table, graph or diagram ("R absorbed the most, 90 ml").
- **Reasoning**: the science rule + what it means ("so it soaks up the most spilt water").
- **Relationship**: "As ___ increases, ___ increases/decreases."
- **Fair test**: change **only one** thing. Aim: "To find out if ___ affects ___."
> MCQ: Read · Circle (NOT, MOST, LEAST, AFTER) · Cross out wrong options · Check every column.
